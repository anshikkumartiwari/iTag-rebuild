Quick Start:
  1. Transfer 'iFind-v1.1.0.apk' to your phone and open it to install.
  2. Enable 'Install from unknown sources' if prompted.
  3. Turn on Bluetooth, launch iFind, and scan for your iTAG tracker.
  4. Enjoy remote camera shutter, reel scrolling, Find My Phone, and lost device alerts!
