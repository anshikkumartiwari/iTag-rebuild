Engineered for Performance & Precision

Every feature in iFind has been rebuilt from the ground up for modern Android versions, ditching bloatware for speed and reliability.

System-Wide Floating Clicker

Place a draggable glowing target reticle anywhere on your screen. When you press the physical iTAG button, iFind dispatches a clean synthetic tap via Android Accessibility. Works inside Motorola Camera, Google Camera, Instagram, TikTok, and Snapchat.

Touch Pass-Through Reel Scroller Accessibility API

Find My Phone with Double-Click

Lost your phone under cushions or in another room? Double-click your iTAG tracker to instantly ring your phone at maximum volume with a repeating alarm and vibration, even if your phone is set to silent or Do Not Disturb.

Foreground Service Max Alarm Volume Continuous Loop

Native CameraX Selfie Viewfinder

Prop your phone up and snap group photos or selfies from up to 30 feet away. Includes customizable 3s/5s countdown timer beeps, flash controls, and instant saving directly to your phone's MediaStore photo gallery.

CameraX 1.4 Countdown Audio Gallery Integration

Find Device (Buzzer Trigger)

Can't find your keys or backpack? Trigger the loud physical buzzer on your iTAG with a single tap. Employs multi-vector fallbacks (standard GATT Immediate Alert 0x1802 and vendor register 0xFFE0) to support all hardware clone revisions.

0x1802 Alert Vendor FFE0 Fallback Instant Buzzer

Disconnect GPS Pinpoint

When your iTAG leaves Bluetooth range, iFind automatically captures your phone's precise GPS coordinates and displays them on a map card with a 1-click shortcut to launch turn-by-turn directions in Google Maps.

Auto GPS Capture Google Maps Intent Range Warning

Deep Hardware Diagnostics

Analyze physical tracker button behavior with millisecond precision. View live hex notification packets from 0xFFE1, packet delta intervals, GATT characteristic trees, and a physical button debounce tester.

Packet Logger GATT Inspector Delta Timing

Quick 3-Minute Installation

Simple sideloading instructions to get up and running on any Android device.

1

Download the APK

Tap the Download APK button above directly on your phone, or download on your PC and transfer via USB/AirDrop.

2

Allow Unknown Apps

Open the downloaded APK from your Files app. If prompted by Android, toggle Allow from this source in Settings to proceed.

3

Pair Your iTAG

Hold the iTAG button for 3s to power it on. Launch iFind, tap Scan Tracker, and pair with 1 tap!
